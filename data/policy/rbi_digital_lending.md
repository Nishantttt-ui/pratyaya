## DL-01 Key Fact Statement must be provided before execution
A Key Fact Statement must be given to the borrower before any loan contract is
executed. It must set out the full cost of the loan, expressed as an annual
percentage rate, along with the recovery mechanism, grievance redressal details
and the cooling-off period. Charges not disclosed in the Key Fact Statement
cannot later be levied on the borrower.

## DL-02 Data collection must be need-based and consented
A lender or its lending service provider may collect only the personal data
actually required to provide the service, and only with the borrower's prior
explicit consent. The purpose of collection must be stated. Consent must be
capable of being withdrawn, and an auditable record of consent must be kept.

## DL-03 Access to device resources is restricted
Digital lending applications must not access mobile phone resources such as the
contact list, call logs, stored files and media, or telephony functions.
One-time access to the camera, microphone or location may be taken where it is
necessary for onboarding or know-your-customer purposes, with the borrower's
explicit consent.

## DL-04 Borrower's right to reject data collection
The borrower must be able to refuse or withdraw consent for the collection of
particular categories of personal data, to require deletion of data already
collected, and to revoke previously granted consent.

## DL-05 Automated credit limits require explicit consent
An automatic increase in a borrower's credit limit without that borrower's
explicit prior consent is prohibited.

## DL-06 Disclosure of lending service providers
The identity of any lending service provider acting on behalf of the regulated
entity must be disclosed to the borrower, along with the details of the recovery
agent who may be authorised to contact them.

## DL-07 Grievance redressal and nodal officer
The regulated entity and its lending service providers must each designate a
nodal grievance redressal officer to deal with complaints about digital lending.
Contact details must be prominently displayed. If a complaint is not resolved
within the stipulated period, the borrower may escalate under the Reserve Bank's
integrated ombudsman scheme.

## DL-08 Algorithmic underwriting must be explainable
Where credit decisioning is automated, the regulated entity remains accountable
for the outcome. Models used for underwriting should be subject to documented
governance, and the entity must be able to explain the basis of a decision. The
use of an automated model does not reduce the lender's obligation to act fairly
or to give reasons.

## DL-09 Cooling-off period
Borrowers must be given a cooling-off or look-up period during which they may
exit the loan by repaying the principal and the proportionate cost, without
paying a penalty.
