## AA-01 The Account Aggregator transfers data but does not read it
An Account Aggregator retrieves and consolidates financial information from
Financial Information Providers and presents it to a Financial Information User
at the customer's instruction. The Account Aggregator itself must not store,
use, analyse or resell the data passing through it.

## AA-02 Consent must be explicit, granular and electronic
Financial information may be shared only under an electronic consent artefact
granted by the customer. The consent must be specific about what is being
shared and with whom, and must be recorded in a standardised, machine-readable
form.

## AA-03 The consent artefact must specify purpose, data types and duration
A consent artefact records the identity of the customer, the nature of the
financial information requested, the purpose for which it is sought, the
identity of the recipient, the frequency of access permitted, and the period
for which the consent remains valid.

## AA-04 Consent is revocable at any time
The customer may revoke a consent artefact at any time through the Account
Aggregator, after which no further data may be pulled under that consent.

## AA-05 Purpose limitation binds the recipient
A Financial Information User may use the data obtained only for the purpose
recorded in the consent artefact. Using consented bank transaction data to
assess a credit application is within purpose; reusing it for an unrelated
product is not.

## AA-06 Customers must be able to see and audit their consents
The customer must be able to view the consents they have granted, the data
accessed under each, and the history of that access.

## AA-07 Alternative data for thin-file assessment
Consented cash-flow information obtained through this framework allows a lender
to assess an applicant who has little or no credit bureau history, by observing
actual income and expenditure behaviour rather than relying on a prior record of
formal borrowing.
