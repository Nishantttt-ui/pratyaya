## DPDP-01 Processing requires consent or a legitimate use
Personal data may be processed only for a lawful purpose, and only either with
the consent of the Data Principal or for a legitimate use recognised by the Act.
A lender assessing a credit application is a Data Fiduciary in respect of the
applicant's personal data.

## DPDP-02 Notice must accompany the request for consent
A request for consent must be accompanied by a notice describing the personal
data to be collected, the purpose of processing, how the Data Principal may
exercise their rights, and how a complaint may be made to the Board.

## DPDP-03 Purpose limitation
Personal data collected for a specified purpose may not be processed for an
unrelated purpose. Data gathered to assess a credit application may not be
repurposed for unrelated marketing without fresh consent.

## DPDP-04 Data minimisation
Only the personal data necessary for the specified purpose may be collected. A
Data Fiduciary should not collect categories of data that are not required for
the stated purpose.

## DPDP-05 Consent is freely revocable
The Data Principal may withdraw consent at any time, and the ease of withdrawal
must be comparable to the ease with which consent was given. On withdrawal, the
Data Fiduciary must cease processing within a reasonable time unless retention
is required by law.

## DPDP-06 Accuracy obligation where data affects a decision
Where personal data is likely to be used to make a decision affecting the Data
Principal, or to be disclosed to another Data Fiduciary, the Data Fiduciary must
ensure that the data is complete, accurate and consistent.

## DPDP-07 Right to access information about processing
The Data Principal may obtain a summary of the personal data being processed and
the processing activities undertaken, together with the identities of other
Data Fiduciaries with whom the data has been shared.

## DPDP-08 Right to correction and erasure
The Data Principal may require the correction of inaccurate or misleading
personal data, the completion of incomplete data, and the erasure of personal
data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

## DPDP-09 Erasure when purpose is served
A Data Fiduciary must erase personal data once the Data Principal withdraws
consent or the specified purpose is no longer being served, unless retention is
necessary for compliance with law.

## DPDP-10 Security safeguards
A Data Fiduciary must take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal
data breach, and must notify the Board and each affected Data Principal in the
event of a breach.
