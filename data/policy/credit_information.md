## CIC-01 Right to access one's own credit information report
An individual is entitled to obtain a copy of the credit information report held
about them by a credit information company, and is entitled to a free full
report once each calendar year.

## CIC-02 Right to have errors corrected
Where a borrower disputes an entry, the credit information company and the
reporting lender must investigate and correct inaccurate information within the
prescribed period. The borrower must be informed of the outcome.

## CIC-03 Access to credit information is purpose-bound
Credit information may be accessed only by a specified user for a permitted
purpose, which includes assessing an application for credit. Access without a
permitted purpose is not allowed.

## CIC-04 Absence of a bureau record is not evidence of poor credit conduct
A person with no prior formal borrowing will have little or no credit
information on record. This reflects an absence of history rather than adverse
history, and the two are distinct. New-to-credit applicants therefore cannot be
assessed on bureau data alone.

## CIC-05 Notification of default reporting
Before reporting a borrower as a defaulter, the lender should give the borrower
notice, so that the borrower has an opportunity to respond.
