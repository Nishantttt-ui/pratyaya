## FPC-01 Reasons for rejection must be communicated
Where a loan application is rejected, the lender must convey the reason for that
rejection to the applicant in writing. This obligation applies irrespective of
whether the assessment was made manually or by an automated model.

## FPC-02 No discrimination in lending
Lenders must not discriminate on grounds such as sex, caste or religion in their
lending activity. Lending decisions must rest on the assessment of credit risk.

## FPC-03 Applications must be acknowledged and time-bound
Loan applications must be acknowledged, and the lender must indicate the period
within which the application will be disposed of.

## FPC-04 Terms must be communicated in writing and in a comprehensible language
The sanctioned amount, the annualised rate of interest, the method of
application of the rate, and all other terms and conditions must be conveyed in
writing, in a language understood by the borrower.

## FPC-05 Transparency in pricing
The rate of interest and the approach to gradation of risk must be disclosed to
the borrower, and published. Risk-based pricing must be explainable by reference
to the risk factors relied on.

## FPC-06 Recovery conduct
Recovery must not involve harassment, intimidation, or contact at unreasonable
hours. Staff and agents must be trained to deal with customers appropriately.
